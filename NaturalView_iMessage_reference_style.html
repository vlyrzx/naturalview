How to Use iMessage Video Studio

Create dark-mode iMessage videos with custom timing, overlays & music

1. Customize Contact & Thread Style

Set the contact name, subtitle, initials or custom photo, status bar clock, and choose between iMessage Blue (#007AFF) and SMS Green (#34C759).

2. Script Messages & Custom Intervals

Click Sent (Me) or Received on any row to toggle sides, write short or multi-line messages, add standalone pauses, reorder by drag-and-drop, and adjust Wait (s), typing speed, and hesitation.

3. Add a Song, Detect Beats & Sync

Upload any MP3/WAV/M4A/OGG track, view detected beat markers on the waveform, click Snap to Beats or Tap to Sync Timings (press Spacebar on each beat), and set fade-in/fade-out and SFX volumes.

4. Pick an Overlay & Export Video

Select a visual overlay (VHS Camcorder, Film Grain, CRT Monitor, Night Vision CCTV, or Soft Glow), adjust camera zoom/drift, and click Download Video to export with audio mixing.
